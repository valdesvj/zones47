---- What it is ----
Zones 47 shows the world's time zones on a dot map of the world. Each standard offset of the official IANA time
zone database (from UTC-12 to UTC+14) is a stop; at each stop it shows the time and date there, the cities of every
country that uses it, and each city's daylight saving time, worked out for today's date (north and south). The
calculator's own zone is marked, so you can read the difference at a glance.

---- How to use it ----
Load ZONE47 and press XEQ ZONE47. The first time it asks TZ, the offset of your calculator's clock from UT in hours
(4 for UT+4, -5.5 for UT-5:30, summer time included); R/S keeps the value shown. It is kept for the next runs.
  4         the stop to the west (a smaller offset)        5 or 6   the stop to the east
  8 2       the city before / after in the stop (each with its own daylight saving)
  0         the end
The header shows the stop's offset, its time and date, your clock and the difference; under the map the city, its
country and its daylight saving now. The column of the stop is highlighted and a triangle marks your own zone. The
time updates every minute while it waits for a key.

---- What it changes on your calculator ----
Your stack and LASTx are given back at the end. It uses the registers R20-R84 and clears them at the end; R00-R19
and R85-R99 are yours. It keeps one variable, TZ (your clock's offset; Almanac 47's MOON47 reads the same TZ).
GRMOD 0 and GRFNT 20 are set at the end.

---- Programs and labels ----
One program, ZONE47, with one global label (ZONE47). The stops (LBL 01-nn, called with XEQ IND), the daylight
saving rules and every routine are local labels. Written with the STRUCT commands, ATEXT and GRFNT of the C47 / R47
firmware (00.109.05.00a0.ALPHA of 5 Oct 2026 or later). The cities and rules come from the computer's tzdata when
the program is built. A city can be added in the program (its stop's list of cities); take care with its daylight
saving rule and the counts, and keep a copy first.
