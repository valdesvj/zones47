      ; A dot map of the world (3 degrees a dot, 81N to 54S) in 24 columns of 15 degrees, UTC-11 at the left
      ; to UTC+-12 at the right (the last column: 172.5E to 172.5W). The official time zones: one stop per
      ; standard offset of the IANA tz database (tzdata, zone1970.tab; tools/make_zones.py -> python/zones.py),
      ; -12 (at sea) to +14, with a city and its daylight saving rule (DST: worked out for the date, added when on).
      ; The column of the stop is highlighted (XOR); the header shows its offset, time and date, and the
      ; calculator's clock (TZ) with the difference; under the map the city and its DST.
      ; Keys: 4 the stop west (a smaller offset), 5 the stop east (6 too), around at the ends; 0 the end.
      ; The time is updated every minute while the program waits for a key.
      ; TZ: the global variable "TZ" = the offset of the calculator's clock from UT in hours (4 for UT+4, -5.5

      ; makes it (0) when it is not there; TZ = 0 (new, or a clock on UT): asked with INPUT, R/S keeps it. The cursor

      ; Screen 400 x 240, row 0 = bottom (as in Almanac 47). ATEXT in GRFNT 21, the small text in GRFNT 10.
