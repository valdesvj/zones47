REM ZONE47 v0.1.0 - Zones 47: the world time zones on the C47 / R47
REM A dot map of the world (3 degrees a dot, 81N to 54S) in 24 columns of 15 degrees, UTC-11 at the left
REM to UTC+-12 at the right (the last column: 172.5E to 172.5W). The official time zones: one stop per
REM standard offset of the IANA tz database (tzdata, zone1970.tab; tools/make_zones.py -> python/zones.py),
REM -12 (at sea) to +14, with a city and its daylight saving (DST, not added to the time: standard time).
REM The column of the stop is highlighted (XOR); the header shows its offset, time and date, and the
REM calculator's clock (TZ) with the difference; under the map the city and its DST.
REM Keys: 4 the stop west (a smaller offset), 5 or 6 the stop east, around at the ends; 0 the end.
REM The time is updated every minute while the program waits for a key.
REM TZ: the global variable "TZ" = the offset of the calculator's clock from UT in hours (4 for UT+4, -5.5
REM for UT-5:30), as MOON47 in Almanac 47: 0 STO+ "TZ" makes it (0) when it is not there. The cursor
REM starts on the stop nearest to TZ, its column marked by a triangle.
REM Screen 400 x 240, row 0 = bottom (as in Almanac 47). ATEXT in GRFNT 21, the small text in GRFNT 10.
REM GRMOD and GRFNT take X (rejig writes "GRMOD r" as GRMOD and the number r: Almanac 47).
REM The user's stack and LASTx are kept (local registers R.00-R.08, as ELEM47 v1.2.0); at the end the
REM work registers R20-R49 are cleared (R-CLR), the other registers are the user's.
REM Map: dot column j (0-119) at x 20 + 3j and 21 + 3j, dot row i (0 the top) at rows 194 - 3i, 193 - 3i.
REM Map column c (0-23: UTC-11 ... UTC+-12) at x 19 + 15c to 33 + 15c, rows 59-196.
REM The stops: LBL 01-nn (written by the build), each pushes the city (text), DST, the column, the offset.
REM Registers: R20-R22 the three bands of a map column (rows 61, 106, 151 up), R23 64 ones, R24 10 ones,
REM R25 the drawing column x, R26 the tick (3 rows), R27 a counter, R30 TZ, R31 the JD of UT now,
